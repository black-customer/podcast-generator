A: Do you spend a lot of time choosing clothes?
B: No, I don't, because I hate doing it. Even if I spent ages choosing clothes, I couldn't guarantee I'd pick the best one — after the choice is made, I'd still regret it, thinking maybe there was something better. So I just don't spend much time on it. I look over the options, pick the best one at the moment based on my feeling, and that's it.
