To some extent, I think it has made our lives easier. But actually, in some ways, technology has made our lives harder — because we have more options now. We need to put real effort into thinking about which action to take. For example, if I want to learn English, there are tons of materials — which one should I use for input? And there are so many methods: shadowing, communicating with people, all kinds of apps. Too many options just make it harder to make a decision and take action. So generally, technology makes life much more convenient — but it also gives our brains more things to think about.
