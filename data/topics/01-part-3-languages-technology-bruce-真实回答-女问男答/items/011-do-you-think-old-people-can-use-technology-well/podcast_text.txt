A: Do you think old people can use technology well?
B: Well, of course it depends. "Old people" is a general concept — age only. Even people of the same age have different learning abilities and different learning skills. Some of them can absolutely use technology well. But if a person never built the habit of learning new things — for example, my grandparents, they'd say, "Oh, I'm old, I don't want to learn new things, I can't learn new things" — then they just make excuses and can't use technology well. So I think it depends on the person's personality. Someone like me — when I'm old, I'll still be following the latest technology trends, because I like learning new things. The future of technology will be brilliant, and I'll definitely be into it and use it well — maybe even better than my own children.
