A: How has modern technology changed the way people experience and interact with music compared to the past?
B: I think it's mostly about the way people communicate with the singer. In the past, if you listened to music, you could only listen — maybe you were surprised by a singer, maybe you liked them, but you couldn't communicate with them. But now, with music apps, you can sometimes interact with those singers directly. It's like reading a book and being able to talk with the writer. And it's also just way more convenient to listen through apps now, instead of cassettes or MP3 players.
