A: Do you think children should have their own smartphones?
B: Yeah, I think it depends on how well the child's self-discipline has developed. There's probably a general age range — like, if you're under 16 or 18, there should be limits on smartphone use. But actually, age doesn't stand for everything. Different children have different parents, and parents' education methods are different. If the parents are good at educating, their children might be more self-disciplined, and then they could have a smartphone earlier. We all know a smartphone is a double-edged sword — if a child can control this sword and use it to benefit their life, then fine. But I think most children don't have that ability yet, so I'd recommend that children at least in primary school should not have their own smartphones. Actually, I got my first smartphone in 2016, when I was about to graduate from primary school and go to junior high.
