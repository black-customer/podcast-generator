The singer I like is J. Cole. I don't know if that's feasible, because he's not exactly a singer — he's a rapper. I don't know if rappers count as singers. But J. Cole is the artist I like. His personality — I think he's honest, kind, and thoughtful. His songs carry so many deep thoughts from his own experience, and he thinks about a lot of things, extracts his thoughts, and passes them on to educate other people — to give the audience power to live on, to live a good life. His music belongs to hip-hop. And why do I like him? Well, first, his songs are amazing — they have rhythm, you can just move your body to them. And you can learn a lot from his lyrics: they give you power, help you handle stress, live a better life, and have the energy to become whoever you want to be.
