Of course, there are a lot of people who can speak foreign languages in China. But from my perspective, in my social circle, I don't actually know a person who can speak two languages fluently. From the internet, though, I know a lot of people who speak many languages — English, of course, Mandarin and Cantonese, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, and so on. And China is a huge country with a huge population. If you just mean "speak," then yes — even I can say a few Japanese words, like Konnichiwa. Because we have this compulsory education system where every student learns English at school, so everybody can speak some English — maybe not fluently, but they can speak. So I think yes.
